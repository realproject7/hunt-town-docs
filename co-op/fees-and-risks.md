# Fees & Risks

## Fees

| Fee | Where it goes |
| --- | --- |
| **Creation fee** (Mint Club's, plus gas) | Mint Club |
| **Mint and burn royalties**, set by the builder | The creator, with a share to the Mint Club protocol |
| **Project update**, a small amount of HUNT | Burned |

Royalties are paid in HUNT. Each project page shows its rates.

## Risks

- **Independent projects.** Co-op tokens are created by their builders. They are not
  operated, controlled or endorsed by the Hunt Town team, and appearing on the Co-op is not a
  recommendation. See [Terms](../terms.md).
- **A steep curve.** Every Co-op token uses the same preset curve, and its price climbs
  steeply as supply grows. Early and late buyers pay very different prices.
- **Royalties on both sides.** A quick round trip returns less HUNT than it cost, unless both
  royalties are 0%.
- **Dollar value follows HUNT.** Prices are set in HUNT, so a token's dollar value moves with
  the HUNT price, even without trades in the token itself.
- **Reserve depth.** Only the HUNT paid into a token backs it, so a token with few buyers has
  a thin reserve.
- **Contracts.** Co-op tokens run on Mint Club V2 contracts, which are audited. Buys paid in
  other tokens also pass through the Co-op's swap contract, which those audits do not cover.
  See [Links & Resources](../reference/links.md#security-and-audits).
