# Builders & Backers

The Co-op runs on two roles. **Builders** create projects; **backers** support them. The
economy is designed so each side needs the other and both share in the upside.

## Builders

Builders launch project tokens backed by HUNT and raise liquidity through the Co-op
economy.

- They issue a token as a **HUNT-backed child token** on bonding-curve mechanics (see
  [Launch a Project Token](launch-a-project-token.md)).
- They gain **early traction and liquidity** as backers mint their token daily, without
  having to bootstrap a market alone.
- As their project grows in activity, **more HUNT locks** into its pool — benefiting the
  builder, HUNT, and the wider Co-op at once.

## Backers

Backers support projects by **minting tokens daily** with their Backing Points (BP), or by
**donating HUNT directly**.

- They hold **Building NFTs**, which give them **backing power** — the amount of support
  they can give builders each day (see [Daily Backing Point (BP)](daily-backing-point.md)).
- They choose which projects to back by minting those projects' tokens with their daily BP.
- They **earn rewards, royalties, and recognition** for the projects they support early.

## Why the two roles reinforce each other

The relationship is mutually beneficial by construction:

- Builders get **demand and liquidity** from backers minting their tokens.
- Backers get **upside and rewards** from backing projects early and well.
- Every mint **locks more HUNT**, so both sides are paid in, and exposed to, the same
  appreciating reserve.

This is the cooperative part of the Co-op: builders and backers are not counterparties in a
zero-sum market but participants in a shared economy that grows for everyone as activity
rises.
