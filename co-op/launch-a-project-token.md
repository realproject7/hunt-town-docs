# Launch a Project Token

Builders join the Co-op by **launching a project token** at
[coop.hunt.town](https://coop.hunt.town). Every Co-op token is a **HUNT-backed child token**
on a bonding curve, issued through the [Mint Club](../mint-club/overview.md) protocol that
underpins much of the ecosystem.

## What launching gives a builder

- **A market from day one.** The token trades on its bonding curve as soon as it launches,
  with no liquidity pool to seed and no listing to wait for.
- **HUNT backing.** The token's reserve is HUNT. As the token's activity grows, more HUNT
  locks into that reserve.
- **Independence.** Each project runs its own curve and its own reserve.

## How it works

1. The builder configures and launches the token.
2. Anyone can buy it with HUNT, which moves HUNT into the token's reserve.
3. The price moves along the bonding curve as supply changes. Selling burns the token and
   returns HUNT from the reserve.

For how bonding curves and their reserves work, see
[Bonding Curves](../mint-club/bonding-curves.md).
