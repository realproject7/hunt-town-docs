## Nature of the protocol

Hunt Town's products — HUNT, Building NFTs, the Co-op, Mint Club, and h402 — are onchain
protocols and applications. Interacting with them involves smart contracts deployed on public
blockchains (Ethereum, Base, and others). All transactions are final and irreversible once
confirmed onchain.

## No financial advice

Nothing in this documentation is financial, investment, legal, or tax advice. Tokens and NFTs
described here (including HUNT, Building NFTs, project tokens, and MINT/MT) may lose value.
Reserve-backing through bonding curves does not guarantee any price, return, or liquidity beyond
what the curve contract provides.

## Risk

Onchain systems carry risk, including smart-contract risk, market risk, bridge risk, and the
risk of total loss. Audits (see [Mint Club Security & Audits](../mint-club/security-audits.md))
reduce but do not eliminate smart-contract risk. Users are responsible for their own wallets,
keys, and decisions.

## Non-custodial

Hunt Town's products are non-custodial where stated (notably h402 payments). Hunt Town does not
hold user private keys. Users are solely responsible for securing their wallets.

## Changes

Products, parameters, and this documentation may change. The canonical, current version of these
terms is published at hunt.town.
