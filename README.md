# Introduction

**Hunt Town is a product studio for the onchain Builder & Agent Economy.**

We build products for the people — and increasingly the agents — building onchain.
Since 2018 we have shipped curation platforms, reward systems, NFT tooling, a
bonding-curve protocol, an onchain cooperative, and an agent-payments network. Some
are still live, some have been retired, and every one of them fed the next.

What ties them together is not a single app but a shared economy. Every product
Hunt Town builds relates back to **HUNT**, the reserve asset that connects the tokens
and NFTs across our ecosystem, and to the people who hold it, build on it, and back
the projects launched within it.

## What we are building now

The studio currently runs three active products. They sit on top of a common
foundation — **HUNT**, **Building NFTs**, and the **Mint Club** bonding-curve
protocol — and each one extends the economy in a different direction.

| Product | What it is | Status |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **h402** | Task-first onchain payments for AI agents and the apps that serve them. | Launching |
| **Co-op** | The onchain cooperative — builders launch HUNT-backed tokens, backers mint them daily. | Live |
| **Mint Club** | The no-code bonding-curve protocol for tokens and NFTs that powers much of the ecosystem. | Live |
