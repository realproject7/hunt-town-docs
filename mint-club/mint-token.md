# MINT Token

**MINT (ticker MT)** is Mint Club's platform token. Importantly for the Hunt Town economy, it
is itself a **HUNT-backed child token**.

## MT and its relationship to HUNT

MT was launched as a **$HUNT child token**: a token issued on a bonding curve with **HUNT in
its reserve**. That means Mint Club's own platform token sits inside the same reserve model
as everything else in the ecosystem: minting MT locks HUNT, and MT's backing is HUNT held in
its curve.

This is the cleanest illustration of the factory's shared-economy thesis: even the protocol
that issues other people's tokens is, at its base, backed by HUNT.

> **History.** Mint Club previously used a token called **MINT**. The **MINT → MT migration
> concluded on June 5, 2025**, after which **MT powers Mint Club exclusively**. References to
> "MINT" in older material refer to the pre-migration token.

## What MT does

- **Buyback & burn.** Mint Club runs an MT **buyback-and-burn** program, using platform
  revenue to buy MT and burn it, tightening supply over time.
- **Parent token.** MT is itself a reserve asset on Mint Club: creators can launch
  MT-backed child tokens and NFTs.

MT has real HUNT in its reserve, and its buybacks run periodically, based on market
conditions.
